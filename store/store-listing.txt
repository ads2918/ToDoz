SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 characters)

A trippy to-do list that floats over any app. Reminders, repeats & confetti!


FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 characters)
--------------------------------------
To Doz is the to-do list that's actually fun to use.

Jot things down in a tap, check them off with a burst of confetti, and shrink the whole list into a little floating bubble that sits over any app — so the next time something pops into your head, your list is one tap away.

★ FLOATS OVER ANY APP
Shrink To Doz into a bubble that stays on screen while you text, browse or watch. Drag it anywhere, tap it to open your list, drop it on the ✕ to close it.

★ PLAN YOUR DAYS
Give tasks a day and time, or leave them loose. The Plan view shows today, tomorrow and the whole week ahead at a glance.

★ REMINDERS THAT GET IT DONE
Get a notification when it's time — then mark it done or snooze it right from the notification. Optional daily summary of today's plan.

★ REPEATING TASKS
Every day, weekdays, every week or every month. Check one off and the next one is ready to go.

★ MAKE IT YOURS
• 40+ backgrounds: solid colors, gradients, tie-dye, psychedelic and dark & bold patterns
• Color shift slider to spin any pattern into new colors
• Living backgrounds: ripple when you touch, flowing colors, melting, rain, lightning storms, kaleidoscope, tunnel and bubbles
• Pick your bubble icon: 👽 👾 🛸 🍄 🦄 👻 🔥 and more

★ STAY ORGANIZED
Optional categories with colors and filters, drag-and-drop to reorder, undo when you delete something by accident.

★ YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything is stored on your device. Save a backup file to Drive or email any time and restore it on a new phone.

Make your to-do list a little more groovy. ✌️
